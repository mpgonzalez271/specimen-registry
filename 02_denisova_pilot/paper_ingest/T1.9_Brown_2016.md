Unfortunately, while many Palaeolithic sites contain large numbers of bones, the majority of these lack the diagnostic features necessary for traditional morphological identification. As a result the recovery of Pleistocene-age human remains is extremely rare. To circumvent this problem we have applied a method of collagen fingerprinting to more than 2000 fragmented bones from the site of Denisova Cave, Russia, in order to facilitate the discovery of human remains. As a result of our analysis a single hominin bone (Denisova 11) was identified, supported through in-depth peptide sequencing analysis and found to carry mitochondrial DNA of the Neandertal type. Subsequent radiocarbon dating revealed the bone to be >50,000 years old. Here we demonstrate the huge potential collagen fingerprinting has for identifying hominin remains in highly fragmentary archaeological assemblages, improving the resources available for wider studies into human evolution." |

**KEY WORKFLOW NOTE — supersession of assignment:**

At the time of this paper (2016), Denisova 11 was interpreted as a Neanderthal on the basis of mtDNA (which is maternally inherited). Only in Slon 2018 (T1.5) — when the nuclear genome was sequenced — was it revealed that Denisova 11's paternal genome was Denisovan, and she was reclassified as a F1 hybrid. **This is a canonical example of `taxonomic_assignment` supersession, and v0 schema must support it via `assignment_history` (append-only log per specimen).**

## v0 schema note (workflow learning)

This paper introduces the **assignment supersession** case. When a later paper (Slon 2018) changes the taxonomic assignment of a specimen based on new evidence (nuclear DNA), the old assignment must remain in the record as a superseded entry, not be deleted. Proposed schema:

```sql
-- specimen_assignment_history
--   specimen_id
--   assignment_publication_id (FK to publications)
--   taxonomic_assignment       (e.g. 'Neanderthal', 'Neanderthal-Denisovan F1 hybrid')
--   assignment_method          (e.g. 'aDNA-mtDNA', 'aDNA-nuclear')
--   effective_from             (date the assignment was published)
--   superseded_by_publication_id (nullable FK — null means current)
```

The `specimens.taxonomic_assignment` field becomes a materialized view of the current (unsuperseded) assignment. Defer to schema-review checkpoint.
