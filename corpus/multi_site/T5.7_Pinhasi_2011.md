**Verification note:** Byline (4 authors) + abstract verified verbatim from PubMed. This paper **resolves the Ovchinnikov 2000 dating discrepancy flagged in block #7 / T5.3**. Ovchinnikov's original ~29 ka claim was superseded by this study's ultrafiltered-collagen AMS date of 39,700 ± 1,100 14C BP (~39 ka cal BP). Bayesian modeling of 16 dates from the LMP layers converges on the same boundary. This paper also resolves whether "late Neanderthal survival" in the Caucasus was real (no) or a dating artifact of contaminated older collagen samples (yes).

**Important disambiguation:** the abstract does not specify whether the "late Neanderthal specimen" is Mezmaiskaya 1 or Mezmaiskaya 2. Common secondary references (including Hajdinjak 2018's Table S1) attribute the ~39 ka date to Mezmaiskaya 2, not Mezmaiskaya 1. This is Michael-verification territory — the paper's supplementary materials will name the exact specimen.
