# Denisova Cave v0 Paper Corpus — Manifest

**Status:** Draft — tier-1 source-locked, tier-2 outline, tier-3 backlog
**Cutoff date:** Publications through 2026-06-30 for v0.1 launch
**Last updated:** 2026-07-26

---

## How this manifest is structured

- **Tier 1 (highest priority, ingest first):** Foundational genome / specimen assignment papers. Every hominin specialist knows these. If SAR gets any of these wrong, credibility is dead. Estimated 12 papers.
- **Tier 2 (core corpus):** Direct-dating, stratigraphy, ZooMS screening, isotope, and secondary genome papers. Estimated 18–25 papers.
- **Tier 3 (context and referenced-elsewhere):** Referenced-but-not-central papers (e.g. faunal analysis, sediment geochemistry, comparative morphology from Xiahe/Baishiya). Estimated 8–15 papers.
- **Non-corpus:** Review papers, popular-science summaries, non-peer-reviewed conference proceedings. Excluded from v0.

**Total v0 target: ~35–50 papers, source-locked, ingested with quoted-passage verification.**

### T1.7 — Douka et al. 2019 (Denisova Cave chronology)

- **DOI:** [10.1038/s41586-018-0870-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0870-z)
- **Full citation:** Douka, K., Slon, V., Jacobs, Z., Ramsey, C.B., Shunkov, M.V., Derevianko, A.P., Mafessoni, F., Kozlikin, M.B., Li, B., Grün, R., Comeskey, D., Devièse, T., Brown, S., Viola, B., Kinsley, L., Buckley, M., Meyer, M., Roberts, R.G., Pääbo, S., Kelso, J., Higham, T. - **Key specimen(s):** Chronological framework for Denisova 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 11
- **Methods reported:** radiocarbon-AMS, U-series (indirectly cited), Bayesian modeling
- **Why tier 1:** Establishes ages for all named Denisova hominin specimens. Critical for the `analyses.analysis_date` and `specimens.stratigraphic_context` fields.
- **Verification status:** metadata source-locked
